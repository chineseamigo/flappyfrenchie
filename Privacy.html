Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Flappy Frenchie ("the Game") handles information when you use the iOS app. For privacy questions, contact enhgame@yahoo.com.

Important: The sections about advertising and in-app purchases apply only if those features are enabled in the version you use. This policy must be checked against the final app build and App Store privacy disclosures before publication.

1. Information handled by the Game

The Game may store gameplay preferences, such as sound settings, selected character, and high scores, on your device. These are intended to support gameplay and do not require you to create an account.

The Game does not ask you to provide your name, address, or email to play. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message.

2. Advertising (if enabled)

If advertising is enabled, the Game may use Google AdMob to display ads, including optional rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and protect ads, subject to your device settings, applicable law, and consent choices.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob guidance for app developers.

If the Game requests permission for tracking on iOS, you may allow or deny that request through Apple's system prompt. You can also manage applicable privacy permissions in iOS Settings.

3. In-app purchases (if enabled)

If the Game offers a purchase to remove advertisements or other optional features, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple may provide transaction and entitlement information needed to recognize or restore purchases. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. How information is used

Information handled by the Game or enabled service providers may be used to maintain game functionality, save preferences, process optional purchases, serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, diagnose technical problems, and respond to support requests.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell information that you send directly to our support email. Where advertising or purchase services are enabled, those providers handle relevant information under their own policies. Locally stored game data may remain on your device until removed through the app or device controls. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve your request. Third-party retention practices are governed by their respective policies.

6. Children's privacy

The Game should not be treated as directed to children solely because it features cartoon dogs. If the Game is marketed to children or submitted to Apple's Kids Category, its advertising, tracking, and data-handling practices must be reviewed and adjusted to meet the stricter applicable requirements. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, contact us at the address below.

7. Your choices

You may choose not to watch optional rewarded ads. You can manage device privacy permissions through iOS Settings, remove locally stored app data using available device controls, and contact us to ask about information you have provided through support. Privacy rights may vary by location.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game's features or service providers change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email enhgame@yahoo.com.
